## ✨ Features & Architecture

1. **Brand Identity & Atmosphere:**
   - Warm vanilla parchment (`#FAF6F0`), deep espresso cocoa (`#241711`), and golden caramel accents (`#D97706`).
   - Elegant typography pairing with serif headlines (*Playfair Display* / *Cormorant Garamond*) and modern clean body text (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).

2. **Hero Section:**
   - Visual presentation of artisan celebration cakes and French viennoiserie.
   - Verified location pill and 1-click **Get Directions** button linking directly to Google Maps.
   - Trust highlights: *4.3★ Rating (500+ local reviews)*, *100% Eggless Daily Bakes*, *2-Hour Express Delivery*, and *15,000+ Cakes Baked*.

3. **Interactive Custom Cake Studio & Price Estimator:**
   - Step-by-step interactive builder allowing customers to select **Occasion**, **Signature Flavor**, **Weight / Size**, **100% Eggless Toggle**, and **Party Add-ons**.
   - Live price calculation with transparent disclaimer.
   - **Confetti celebration trigger** and 1-click **WhatsApp Inquiry** pre-formatting the exact cake specifications to `+91 97043 03881`.

4. **Digital Menu & Cafe Bites Explorer:**
   - Live search by item name or ingredient.
   - Category tabs: *Artisanal Cakes*, *French Patisserie & Desserts*, *Charlie Bistro & Starters*, and *Shakes & Artisan Coffees*.
   - Quick dietary filters: *100% Eggless Only*, *Chef's Picks*, and *Best Sellers*.
   - Real, verified prices from the Karimnagar menu (Opera Pastry ₹99, Caramelized Banana Waffles ₹150, Charlie Chicken ₹260, Veg Chew Rollers ₹200, Fudge Brownie Shake ₹180, Cappuccino ₹90).

5. **Interactive Slide-over Order Tray (Cart):**
   - Real-time quantity adjustments, subtotal estimation, and 1-click **Place Order on WhatsApp** button.

6. **The Story of La'Delicious & Charlie Restaurant:**
   - Highlighting the sweet tooth patisserie craftsmanship alongside the savory continental bistro experience.

7. **Table & Celebration Party Booking:**
   - Reserve seating or private celebration area for birthdays, anniversaries, and family get-togethers in Jyothinagar.

8. **Google Reviews & Community Trust:**
   - Real customer feedback highlights with verified badges and direct links to leave or read reviews on Google Maps.

9. **Interactive Location, Maps Directions & Contact Section:**
   - Embedded interactive map centered on Karimnagar Jyothinagar.
   - Working turn-by-turn driving directions button.
   - Direct click-to-call phone buttons and quick inquiry form.

10. **Mobile-First Responsiveness:**
    - Fixed mobile bottom quick-action bar with **Call Us**, **Cake Studio**, **WhatsApp**, and **Cart Counter**.
    - Hamburger navigation drawer with smooth scroll anchors.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler:** Vite 8
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Celebration Effects:** Canvas Confetti
